Controls

←→ Steer the front wheels. When you let go, they return to straight as the car rolls.
↑↓ Drive forward / reverse. The opposite key brakes first.
R Restart the scenario.
On a phone, use the on-screen buttons: steering bottom-left, pedals bottom-right.

Park fully inside the dashed box, facing the arrow, and stop. Hold still for a second. Bumping into things stops the car; back away to fix it.

Every finished level gets a ✓. Finish with no crashes and no more reversals (changes between forward and reverse) than the level's par to earn a ★.
